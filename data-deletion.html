How to delete your data

F.W.D.'s tool (Genesis Orbs / Jarvis) keeps comments and messages that people sent to the business's Facebook Page, Instagram, Threads and TikTok accounts, on the owner's own computer. To have what we hold about you deleted:

  1. Email genesis@goldengoalglobal.us with the subject "Delete my data".
  2. Tell us which account you wrote to and your name or username on that platform, so we can find it.
  3. We will delete the matching comments, messages and related records and reply to confirm. We aim to do this within 30 days.

If you signed in with Facebook, Instagram, Threads or TikTok

You can also remove the app's access yourself in that platform's settings (for Facebook and Instagram: Settings > Apps and Websites; for TikTok: Settings and privacy > Security and permissions > Apps and services permissions). Then email us to delete anything still stored.

More detail is in our Privacy Policy.
