Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 26, 2026

F.W.D. ("we", "us") runs a private tool called Genesis Orbs (also known as Jarvis). It helps the owner of F.W.D. manage the business's own Facebook Page, Instagram account, Threads account and TikTok account. It is not a public app: it is used only by the owner of F.W.D., on the owner's own computer, for the owner's own accounts.

What the tool accesses

After the owner signs in and gives permission on each platform, the tool can access, for the owner's own accounts only:

  • Comments on our posts and videos, and the names and profile names of the people who wrote them.
  • Messages sent to our Facebook Page and Instagram account, and the names of the people who sent them.
  • Basic statistics for our accounts and posts (for example followers, views, likes).
  • The posts, photos and videos we publish through the tool.

We do not collect data from anyone's personal profile beyond what they send to or post on our accounts, and the tool does not look people up or build profiles of them.

How we use it

  • To show the owner new comments, messages and statistics, and to alert the owner (for example by a Telegram message to the owner's own phone).
  • To help the owner write draft replies and posts. A draft is shown to the owner first. Nothing is posted, sent or deleted without the owner's approval.

Comments and messages may be processed by an AI service (Claude, from Anthropic) to summarize them and suggest drafts. We do not use this data to advertise to anyone, and we do not sell it.

Where it is stored

Access tokens that the platforms give the tool are stored in the Windows Credential Manager on the owner's computer, not in files and not in chat. Comments, messages and statistics are kept on the owner's computer only for as long as needed to run the tool, and the owner can delete them at any time.

Who we share it with

We do not sell or rent personal data. Data goes only to: the platform it came from (Meta, TikTok), the AI service described above when the owner asks for a summary or draft, and the owner's own Telegram account for alerts. We may disclose data if the law requires it.

Your choices and deleting your data

If you wrote to or commented on one of our accounts and want us to delete what we hold about you, see our data deletion page or email genesis@goldengoalglobal.us. The owner can also disconnect the tool at any time from the settings of each platform (Facebook, Instagram, Threads, TikTok), which ends its access.

Children

The tool is not meant for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

We may update this policy and will change the effective date above when we do.
